Mélissa

Mélissa is a personal organizer for Android by Kenaz 8. Tasks, calendar, Eisenhower matrix, Pomodoro timer, subscriptions, habits, events and finances in one app, with no ads and no analytics.

Google Calendar sync (optional)

If you connect your Google account, Mélissa reads, creates, updates and deletes events in your primary Google Calendar so that they appear next to your tasks. The app requests only the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar.events permission. Data goes directly between your device and Google; the developer does not receive or store it. You can disconnect in the app settings and revoke access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Privacy
